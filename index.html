Scene 1: The Crib on Apollo-99

🛸

01. My crib on Apollo-99

I was born aboard orbital research station Apollo-99, rocked to sleep by gentle lunar gravity and the hum of the life-support engines. Instead of a wooden mobile, I watched real shooting stars streak past my nursery viewport.

🌌

02. My first float outside the air-lock

During my very first zero-gravity crawl, curiosity proved stronger than any safety tether. I drifted into a shimmering cloud of stardust and learned that weightlessness is just the universe’s way of giving everyone room to fly.

🛰️

03. My starlight repair station

I packed my tiny magnetic wrench and a spool of glowing constellation wire, set up shop on an abandoned satellite dish, and tuned radio frequencies so lullabies from Earth could reach the furthest moons.

🪐

04. My bedtime in zero gravity

Now, when ground control signals lights-out, I click my helmet comms to Sleep Mode, float into my tethered sleeping cocoon, and watch Earth glow like a soft blue nightlight. I whisper goodnight to the stars and dream in the quiet dark.
